LUMEN v5 — revision notes
Revised 9 October 2026 from Draft/v4. The v4 files are preserved.

Editorial changes

Daniel Yoon remains the initiating NovaMind software engineer throughout. His
private fate and the substituted custody corpse are distinguished explicitly.
Priya is alive when the role is offered to Adrian; her assassination and the
subsequent cardiac-arrest cover story now occur in a consistent order.

Seb is shot at the Dutch farmhouse, which is also the location of his last
meeting with Adrian. Raven's subsequent ambush takes place in an Arnhem hotel.
Her wounds, age references, loyalties, and later movements are consistent.
Ólafur knows the handoff drive is a backup; Adrian's later account reflects that.
Tomás's sister is Ana, a journalist killed in Cambodia in 2019. The competing
names and death accounts have been reconciled.

Earlier Kai scenes retain ambiguous clues without explicitly disclosing his
authorship before the café confession. His age and the earlier photographs are
consistent with the founding date and estrangement. Other reused names and
cast-count discrepancies have been repaired.

The final operation follows one fixed Tuesday 12:17:03 CET activation time.
Countdowns, local time zones, travel, the Copenhagen handoff, and epilogue
weekdays have been reconciled. The Belgian mirror cache replaces the impossible
last-minute delivery from Copenhagen. Seven targets have explicit deployment
coverage, including Polk. Trigger arrival, warning delays, local root completion,
and subsequent data delivery are distinct stages with consistent timings.

Chapter numbers now run continuously from 1 through 89. Chapter-map.json records
the old-to-new numbering. Repetitive explanations, premature revelations, draft
residue, and contradictory logistics have been trimmed while retaining the
book's reflective voice. The manuscript is 138,075 whitespace-delimited words,
down from 146,995: a reduction of 8,920 words (about six percent).

Deliverables and checks

LUMEN.docx is rebuilt from the five revised Markdown parts. All 4,409 source
paragraphs other than separators and front-matter metadata match the Word text.
The final 499-page render has been visually checked, either directly or through
pixel-identical comparison with already inspected pages. Chapter-ending dividers
that caused nearly empty pages were omitted. A few short chapter tails remain;
they do not affect the narration export.

The audiobook folder contains a full narration transcript, front matter,
89 chapter transcripts, and 241 paragraph-bounded chunks of at most 700 words.
The export uses spoken countdowns and technical labels and keeps performance
directions separate from the transcript. Source hashes match the manuscript.
The narration guide supplies cast identities and working pronunciation choices.

Audiobook cost estimate

Model: gemini-3.8-flash-lite-tts. Paid Gemini Developer API rates checked
9 October 2026. No audio has been generated and no API charges have been incurred.

The narration export has 137,770 words. At 150 words per minute it runs about
15.3 hours before additional pauses. Estimated single-pass cost is USD 8.39
standard or USD 4.20 batch. With 30 percent additional generation for retakes,
the estimates are USD 10.91 and USD 5.45. A practical allowance is USD 15
standard or USD 8 batch. These are estimates, not billing caps.

At 130–170 words per minute, the first pass is approximately USD 7.42–9.66
standard or USD 3.71–4.83 batch. Text token counts are estimated; audio billing
depends on actual duration. Taxes, editing, mastering, and distribution are
excluded. Google's published rates double on 1 January 2027.
