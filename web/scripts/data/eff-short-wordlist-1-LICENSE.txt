Lumen selection: the original 1,296-row file is unchanged. The generator omits
the single hyphenated entry "yo-yo" and retains the original entry "yoyo".
All 1,295 selected words are unique original lowercase a-z words of 3-5 letters.
Six independent uniform selections use hyphens as separators. This filter does not
imply endorsement by EFF or the original author.
